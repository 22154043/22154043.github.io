Types of phones

Online forum

Phones are a important part of life for most people, it lets you call people, find information via WWW(World Wide Web) like YouTube, Telephones were invented in 1876 and progressed into the phones you know today, mobile phones have evolved from bulky, expensive "bricks" in the 1980s into pocket sized smartphones that function as personal computers. The first handheld cellular call was made by Motorolas Martin Cooper in 1973, but commercial and portable phones only arrived in the 1980s and today these devices are ubiquitous, with over 5.4 billion people using them to access the internet, communicate and take photos. some of the more influential and most known phones include the Motorola DynaTAC 8000X (1984):The first commercial handheld "brick" phone. Nokia 3310(2000):Famed for its extreme durability and for popularising the snake game. Nokia 1100(2003):The best-selling mobile phone of all time, with over 250M units sold, and more. Key Milestones include 1992:First SMS (text messages) sent. 1999:First phone with a built in camera (Kyocera VP-210) 2003:Blackberry 6210, pioneering mobile email and 2019:Introduction of 5G networks and foldable screens.
