Samsung

Samsung is a South Korean multinational conglomerate, starting in 1939 as a small trading company by Lee Byung-CHull and growing into a global technology leader known for electronics, smartphones and more. Samsung is mostly known for innovative consumer electronics, especially Galaxy smartphones, tablets, smart TVs and home appliances, alongside being a leading manufacturer of semiconductors(memory chips) etc.
