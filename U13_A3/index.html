Samsung is a South Korean multinational conglomerate that started in 1938 as a small trading company, that grew into a global technology leader known mostly for its electronics (smartphones, TVs, appliances).

Apple Inc. is a global giant, founded by Steve jobs, Steve wozniak, and Ronald Wayne in 1976. known for revolutionary consumer electronics such as iPhone, iPad and Mac computers, along with software (iOS, macOS and services such as apple store and music.

Android is a popular open source, Linux based operating system (OS) developed by Google primarily for mobile devices like smartphones and tablets, but also powering smart TVs, cars and watches.
